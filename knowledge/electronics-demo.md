Troubleshooting guide for Pulse Wireless Earbuds not pairing: First, place both earbuds back into the charging case and close the lid for 10 seconds to power cycle them. Second, make sure the charging case has battery power (the LED indicator should light up). Third, go to your phone or computer Bluetooth settings and select "Forget" or delete "Pulse Earbuds" from your paired devices list. Fourth, open the charging case lid, take both earbuds out simultaneously, and wait 5 seconds for them to link to each other before tapping "Pulse Earbuds" in your device's Bluetooth menu. If this doesn't fix it, contact support@nexora.example.

Troubleshooting guide for Boom Mini Bluetooth Speaker not charging: First, ensure you are using the included USB-C cable and a functional 5V/2A wall power adapter. Second, inspect the speaker USB-C charging port for lint, debris, or moisture, and gently clear it with a dry toothpick or compressed air. Third, try plugging into an alternative wall outlet or USB port to rule out a faulty adapter. Fourth, press and hold the Power button for 10 seconds while connected to the charger to perform a hardware reset. If this doesn't fix it, contact support@nexora.example.

Troubleshooting guide for Crisp Air Fryer not turning on: First, ensure the power cord is firmly plugged into a dedicated, working 120V electrical wall outlet. Second, confirm that the removable fry basket is completely pushed in until it clicks flush into the main housing, as the unit features a safety interlock micro-switch that prevents power when the basket is ajar. Third, check whether your kitchen wall outlet circuit breaker has tripped. If the LED display still fails to illuminate, contact support@nexora.example.

Troubleshooting guide for Glide Robot Vacuum not connecting to Wi-Fi: First, ensure your home Wi-Fi network router is broadcasting on the 2.4GHz frequency band, as smart home vacuums cannot connect to 5GHz-only networks. Second, place the robot vacuum within 3 meters (10 feet) of your Wi-Fi router during initial setup. Third, press and hold the power and home buttons simultaneously on the vacuum for 5 seconds until you hear the voice prompt "Wi-Fi reset". Fourth, restart your mobile app and follow the on-screen pairing prompts. If this doesn't fix it, contact support@nexora.example.

Troubleshooting guide for Whirl Countertop Blender leaking from base: First, always unplug the blender base from the electrical outlet before inspecting. Second, inspect the rubber sealing silicone gasket ring at the blade assembly to verify it is seated flat and snugly in its groove without twists or tears. Third, ensure the jar collar is threaded tightly and squarely onto the glass or pitcher base. Fourth, check that liquids are not filled past the 1.5L MAX fill line. If this doesn't fix it, contact support@nexora.example.

Troubleshooting guide for Pulse Fit Smartwatch not syncing data: First, verify that Bluetooth is turned on and active on your smartphone. Second, ensure the companion smartwatch app is open or permitted to run in the background without aggressive battery-saver restrictions. Third, toggle your phone's Bluetooth off for 5 seconds and turn it back on. Fourth, restart both your smartphone and the Pulse Fit Smartwatch by holding the side crown button for 5 seconds and tapping Restart. If this doesn't fix it, contact support@nexora.example.
