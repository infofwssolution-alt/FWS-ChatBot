# Veloura Skin — Knowledge Base Chunks

Gentle Cloud Cleanser is a gentle cream-gel cleanser priced at $18 for 150 ml (5.1 fl. oz.). It is formulated for dry, sensitive, and normal skin types. Key ingredients are colloidal oat extract, ceramide NP, and panthenol. How to use: massage 1-2 pumps onto damp skin morning and night, rinse with lukewarm water. It has no known contraindications and is safe for daily use on compromised barriers.

Clear Balance Gel Cleanser is a purifying foaming gel cleanser priced at $19 for 150 ml (5.1 fl. oz.). It is formulated for oily, acne-prone, and combination skin types. Key ingredients are zinc PCA, tea tree hydrosol, and green tea extract. How to use: lather a nickel-sized amount with water and massage over face for 60 seconds morning and night, then rinse. Those with severely dry or flaky skin should avoid it as it is designed for oil control.

Glow Vitamin C Serum is a radiance serum priced at $34 for 30 ml (1.0 fl. oz.). It is formulated for all skin types dealing with dullness, dark spots, and uneven tone. Key ingredients are 15% stabilized vitamin C (ethyl ascorbic acid), ferulic acid, and kakadu plum extract. How to use: apply 3-4 drops every morning to cleansed, dry skin before moisturizer and sunscreen. Who should avoid it: do not combine in the same application step with retinol; use vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night.

Calm Niacinamide 10% Serum is a pore-refining and soothing serum priced at $28 for 30 ml (1.0 fl. oz.). It is formulated for oily, acne-prone, combination, and sensitive skin. Key ingredients are 10% niacinamide (vitamin B3), 1% zinc PCA, and centella asiatica (cica). How to use: press 3-4 drops into clean face and neck morning and evening before moisturizer. It is well-tolerated by almost all skin types; individuals with an isolated sensitivity to concentrated niacinamide should patch test first.

Hydra Hyaluronic Serum is an intensive multi-depth hydration serum priced at $30 for 30 ml (1.0 fl. oz.). It is formulated for all skin types, including dry, dehydrated, oily, and sensitive skin. Key ingredients are multi-molecular weight hyaluronic acid complex, provitamin B5 (panthenol), and snow mushroom extract. How to use: apply 3-4 drops onto slightly damp skin morning and evening, immediately sealing with moisturizer. Universally tolerated with no contraindications.

Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum is an anti-aging and resurfacing night treatment priced at $38 for 30 ml (1.0 fl. oz.). It is formulated for normal, dry, combination, and oily skin concerned with fine lines and uneven texture. Key ingredients are 0.3% encapsulated retinol, plant squalane, and bisabolol. How to use: evening use only; apply 1-2 drops twice a week on dry skin, gradually increasing frequency as tolerated; always follow with a barrier moisturizer. Who should avoid it: not for sensitive skin, compromised eczema flare-ups, or broken skin. Anyone who is pregnant or nursing should not use retinol and should consult their physician.

Cloud Barrier Cream is a deep barrier-recovery moisturizer priced at $32 for 50 ml (1.7 oz.). It is formulated for dry, sensitive, and normal skin types. Key ingredients are a 5-ceramide biomimetic complex (EOP, NS, NP, AS, AP), plant squalane, and colloidal oatmeal. How to use: warm a dime-sized amount between fingertips and press into face and neck morning and night. Very oily or severe acne-prone skin in high humidity may find it too rich.

Weightless Water Gel is an oil-free burst moisturizer priced at $29 for 50 ml (1.7 oz.). It is formulated for oily, combination, and acne-prone skin types. Key ingredients are fermented bamboo water, aloe leaf juice, and sodium hyaluronate. How to use: smooth a blueberry-sized amount over face and neck morning and night after serums. Very dry skin may need richer lipid moisture during winter.

Daily Shield SPF 50 Fluid is a weightless invisible chemical sunscreen priced at $26 for 50 ml (1.7 fl. oz.). It is formulated for all skin types (normal, combination, oily, dry). Key ingredients are broad spectrum UV filters SPF 50, white tea extract, and vitamin E. How to use: apply generously as the last step in your morning routine 15 minutes before sun exposure (two finger lengths for face and neck). Daytime use only; rinse clean at night.

Mineral Sheer SPF 30 is a 100% mineral non-nano zinc oxide sunscreen priced at $27 for 50 ml (1.7 fl. oz.). It is formulated specifically for sensitive, reactive, and post-procedure skin. Key ingredients are 18% non-nano zinc oxide, chamomile flower water, and allantoin. How to use: blend two finger lengths evenly across face and neck every morning. Completely free of chemical UV filters and synthetic fragrance.

Clay Reset Mask is a purifying wash-off mask priced at $24 for 75 ml (2.5 fl. oz.). It is formulated for oily, acne-prone, and congested skin. Key ingredients are French green clay, white kaolin clay, spirulina algae, and evening primrose oil. How to use: apply an even layer to clean skin, leave for 10-15 minutes without letting it fully crack, and rinse gently with warm water. Use 1-2 times weekly. Do not use on the same night as Renew Retinol; avoid on very dry or irritated skin.

Overnight Dew Mask is a leave-on sleeping mask priced at $31 for 75 ml (2.5 fl. oz.). It is formulated for dry, dull, mature, and normal skin types. Key ingredients are copper tripeptide-1, fermented black bee honey extract, and beta-glucan. How to use: apply a smooth layer over face and neck as the last step of your evening routine 2-3 times weekly; rinse with warm water the next morning. Oily complexions can use it as a 20-minute rinse-off glow treatment instead.

Ingredient pairing rules: Vitamin C and Retinol should never be applied at the same time. Use Glow Vitamin C Serum in the morning to protect against free radicals and use Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum in the evening to stimulate cell turnover.

Ingredient pairing rules: Retinol should not be combined with exfoliating clay masks or strong chemical acids on the same evening. When using Renew Retinol 0.3% Night Serum, do not use Clay Reset Mask on that same night. Always use sunscreen (such as Daily Shield SPF 50 or Mineral Sheer SPF 30) during the day when using retinol.

Ingredient pairing rules: Niacinamide and Hyaluronic Acid are universally compatible. Calm Niacinamide 10% Serum and Hydra Hyaluronic Serum can be layered safely together morning and night. Calm Niacinamide can also be applied before Renew Retinol at night to help buffer irritation.
